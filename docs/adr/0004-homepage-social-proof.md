# Social proof lives on the homepage

The site shows two kinds of proof: the brands Flavia has worked with, and her Google reviews. Both have changed home more than once, so this records where they settled.

**Decision:** there is no Collaborazioni page. Loghi partner render in the homepage Sezione Dicono di me. Punteggio Google and Recensioni Google render in the review strip of Sezione Tour. All of it is curated content on the `homepage` entry, shared across locales, with reviews kept in their original language. Nothing is fetched from Google.

**Rejected:**

- A Collaborazioni landing that owned the logos and quotes, with the homepage reading from it.
- Testimonianze, replaced by Google-style reviews.
- A live Google Business or Places integration.
