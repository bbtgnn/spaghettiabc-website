# Social proof lives on the homepage

The site shows two kinds of proof: the brands Flavia has worked with, and her Google reviews. Both have changed home more than once, so this records where they settled.

**Decision:** there is no Collaborazioni page. Loghi partner render in the homepage Sezione Dicono di me. Punteggio Google and Recensioni Google render in the review strip of Sezione Tour, shared across locales, with review text kept in its original language.

**Rejected:**

- A Collaborazioni landing that owned the logos and quotes, with the homepage reading from it.
- Testimonianze, replaced by Google-style reviews.

**Status:** accepted. The earlier “nothing is fetched from Google / curated only on `homepage`” claim is superseded by [ADR-0008](0008-google-reviews-plugin.md).
