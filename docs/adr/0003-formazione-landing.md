# Formazione is one landing

Formazione is Flavia's B2B offer: percorsi and incontri for companies and professionals.

**Decision:** Formazione is one landing at `/training`, read from the single `training` entry ([ADR-0001](0001-single-entry-collections.md)); individual percorsi are copy inside that page. The homepage Sezione Formazione deliberately owns its own title, subtitle, image, and CTA label instead of referencing the landing, so the teaser is edited in the Homepage form and can say something different from the landing.

**Rejected:** a catalogue of percorsi as separate entries, until one is decided; a reference from the homepage to the `training` entry.
